Quick sentence map  ·  Your rule is a great travel default: [topic] は → [object] を → verb. Japanese verbs normally come at the end, and a known subject is often omitted. Read the particles specially: は is wa as the topic marker; を is usually o as the object marker. Example: ビールをください = “Beer, please.” Today’s 10-minute drill: read the chart twice, cover the romaji and recall one row, then say one travel phrase to a real person.
